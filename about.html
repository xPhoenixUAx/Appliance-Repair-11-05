About Appliance Atlas FAQ.

A few practical answers about how the site works, what it does, and what independent providers are responsible for.

Does Appliance Atlas perform appliance repairs directly?

No. Appliance Atlas is an aggregator and coordination service. Repair work, diagnosis, estimates, scheduling, warranties, and service outcomes are handled by independent local providers.

Why are services grouped by appliance system?

Different appliances require different diagnostics. A refrigerator cooling failure, a dryer airflow issue, an oven ignition problem, and a dishwasher drain issue all need different intake questions and preparation.

How does the site help homeowners before a visit?

The site helps organize the request by appliance type, symptom, urgency, access details, and likely diagnostic path, making the handoff to a provider clearer.

Should homeowners verify licenses and insurance?

Yes. Homeowners are responsible for verifying that any hired contractor or provider has the licenses, insurance, and qualifications required for the work being performed.
